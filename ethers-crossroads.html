Getting Started

Ether's Crossroads is a card game for two players. Each player brings a deck of playing cards with both Jokers, and the table needs a handful of dice: d4, d6, d8, d12 and d20. Cards are soldiers, knights and royalty placed on a field of lines; dice decide every fight; and the dead become souls that can be spent as mana on spells, or raised again from the shared grave.

  1. Draw. Each player draws 2d4 cards from their own shuffled deck. A hand holds six cards at most.
  2. Roll for the opening. Both players roll. The highest roll sets up their cards on their own lines first, then the other player sets up. The highest roller also takes the first turn.
  3. Take turns. Every turn runs Optional Draw → Prep → Attack. In the draw phase, roll a d4 as many times as you like and draw that many cards. In the prep phase, place, move and sacrifice cards and cast spells. In the attack phase, each of your stacks may attack once. The very first turn of the game has no draw phase.
  4. Fight with dice. A stack fights with the score of its best card: the card's value plus its die. Stacking cards does not add their scores together, but every card in a stack is another hit it can take.
  5. Gather souls. Sacrificed cards become your soul pile, worth up to six mana, which pays for spells such as Divine Odds, Clairvoyance or a King Conjure. Once a turn, the free Grave Blessing lets you roll for the dead.
  6. Win. Destroy every card the other player has on the field, or leave them with no deck, no hand and no soul pile when their turn ends: an economic victory.

The full rules, including every card's role, the stacking formula and all the spells, are under All Rules. Play Game runs the game in the browser for two players sharing one device.
