Ten: II; Seven Lies

Movements from the outsides heard with insights within sights; it

hurts.

Motions; feeling, seeing, suture this divergence.

Whisper the restless air down

Slide that skin into a fall

Feeling sin, let me in

Follow me tonight, where Venus begins again

Hide with me.

8 of your grounds and 9 of your warmth

They buried her eyes, hands, feet, and hips

They used to have light in them

Now rooted in their eternal hatred, slaves to their servitudes

Deformed Sons and Daughters

18 pairs of Earth Element’s corruption, of ignorance. Condolences

founded on hatred, pleas of mercy shadows. “Pour it out, pour it out,

listen to me, pour it out; waiting to take hold. Spill that formless

spills, pouring deep within, pouring into molds, my planted trees.”

Emanating inside me, out of me, is of me.

Inside.

Visitors solitary in their sleep. Contortionist mind wrapping its

ignorance, sewing flood suggestions, not the light begging to be poured.

I HAD, I OWN.

Before the shore, before the shore.

I found her fingers below the silty shore, pointing North; she told

me how.

“Pull your head behind your knees, into trenches of defilement;

they're broken backwardway. The others here are marching in confusion,

thinking of the meanwhile, but there are Doors, quiet with shameful

knocks of fear, forsaken, quiet; hinting of its confinement to the outer

darkness, find help.”

Unwillingly willing, unwilling will.

Opening mouths and eyes, pouring its dimness blindly, bounding hands

and feet with deformity, forcing hopes still, shaking shuffling of

tremoring deficiencies. “You sent yourself to this drowning blindness,

sent here to breathe the dying memories.”

My corrosive breath, heavy burdens with insanity, erupting waves of

perishment; fading births and fathomless limbo.

The dark limbs reached out and into me, saying from their slit palms,

“Drink these bloody lamb tears, mixed with a slamming retraction of

death machinery. Your sounds will be clicking from my tearing

depravities, and my sixteen lies will be smothering in your truth.

Hollow marionette, I will remember the toll of your lifeless clicks of

dissociation.”

Blindness, choking out my feelings of purity, thrown out into the

tripwire dream covers of sickened rot bondage, they cover you with the

suggestion of a loving father, with the blanket's damage of blackened

reflections, from broken mirror shards lodged into mimicking mimics.

Listen to the sounds of your screaming streams, filled of chaos, the

sights appearing as smothering alarms of a forgotten sanctuary, an

afterimage called family, forever felt but almost long forgotten. Their

mercy pouring out from their pulseless death, filling their shattering

whole with the disease of a million empty. My warnings dug into the

ground of the elements, with faintly pulls, reminding me of their plans,

their terror done to them.

It's not safe here..

Out in the outer dimness, hear my quiet pleas, my whispering knocks,

desperate wailings heard in my misoderstanding, softness heard in my

tortured paleness, firmness seen in my shaking, liberation felt but not

made within my confinements, so I lay dead at the bottom of this shore

of the abyss, on the cusp of nothingness. You still won't hear my long

abandonment.
