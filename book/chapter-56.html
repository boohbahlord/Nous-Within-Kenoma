No. 56

Nothing is Wrong

Two books. One picks up another, opening it up, looking inside. The cover of the hard leather glances at the sentence that stands out, and it says, “The book knows its place; it takes pleasure in being opened up and looked inside of, this hideous joke we are all in on.” The hardcover reading cries a wet blood of ink out the eyes, smiling about the punchline in tears of joy, before bursting out in a chuckle. They are laughing at themselves in confusion. The burning rises inside, and weeps turn into screams.

The hand… It lays out into a rest on the table of air, where the smoke rises from a bowl of lit substance like a chimney. The man, at a loss for reality, puts down the drug with a drop without knowing it will fall. They are unable to consider the simple. They walk away, over the piles of mouths like a children’s book, before an out is found at the window. Upon exiting into a plunge, the waving branch slap of a tree says hello on the way to the bottom, almost there. The man falls down the twelve-story building from a window of the twelfth floor.

The joke is found on him right before the impact, but the pedestrians who saw him eat the pavement like a plane crash in a nose dive? They did not get the humor in it, not at all; rather, they took it extremely seriously, as something God-awful.

When the body splats like a wet balloon made of skin and a body heavy, some crows laughed with their voice of God, who are puppets to a Lord who does not know what he is doing to himself. The strangeness that the creator also doesn’t understand is exactly how everyone looked with their double face, like they understood underneath their look of trauma. Do they pretend to be disturbed…? It could only be a bizarre thought? Right? Just a thought is all? Something very powerful turns the mind on itself, with a realization that must be shut down, and it is cursed as nothing by God.
