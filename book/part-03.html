Deformed Sons and Daughters

18 pairs of Earth Element’s corruption, of ignorance. Condolences founded on hatred, pleas of mercy shadows. “Pour it out, pour it out, listen to me, pour it out; waiting to take hold. Spill that formless spills, pouring deep within, pouring into molds, my planted trees.” Emanating inside me, out of me, is of me.

Inside.

Visitors solitary in their sleep. Contortionist mind wrapping its ignorance, sewing flood suggestions, not the light begging to be poured. I HAD, I OWN.

Before the shore, before the shore.

I found her fingers below the silty shore, pointing North; she told me how.

“Pull your head behind your knees, into trenches of defilement; they're broken backwardway. The others here are marching in confusion, thinking of the meanwhile, but there are Doors, quiet with shameful knocks of fear, forsaken, quiet; hinting of its confinement to the outer darkness, find help.”

Unwillingly willing, unwilling will.

Opening mouths and eyes, pouring its dimness blindly, bounding hands and feet with deformity, forcing hopes still, shaking shuffling of tremoring deficiencies. “You sent yourself to this drowning blindness, sent here to breathe the dying memories.”

My corrosive breath, heavy burdens with insanity, erupting waves of perishment; fading births and fathomless limbo.

The dark limbs reached out and into me, saying from their slit palms, “Drink these bloody lamb tears, mixed with a slamming retraction of death machinery. Your sounds will be clicking from my tearing depravities, and my sixteen lies will be smothering in your truth. Hollow marionette, I will remember the toll of your lifeless clicks of dissociation.”

Blindness, choking out my feelings of purity, thrown out into the tripwire dream covers of sickened rot bondage, they cover you with the suggestion of a loving father, with the blanket's damage of blackened reflections, from broken mirror shards lodged into mimicking mimics.

Listen to the sounds of your screaming streams, filled of chaos, the sights appearing as smothering alarms of a forgotten sanctuary, an afterimage called family, forever felt but almost long forgotten. Their mercy pouring out from their pulseless death, filling their shattering whole with the disease of a million empty. My warnings dug into the ground of the elements, with faintly pulls, reminding me of their plans, their terror done to them.

It's not safe here..

Out in the outer dimness, hear my quiet pleas, my whispering knocks, desperate wailings heard in my misoderstanding, softness heard in my tortured paleness, firmness seen in my shaking, liberation felt but not made within my confinements, so I lay dead at the bottom of this shore of the abyss, on the cusp of nothingness. You still won't hear my long abandonment.
