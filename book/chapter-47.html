Chapter 47

Gutted Hand

“Yes to the suggestion…” Kether lazily growls with a crooked look of bother, waving them away with the back of his hand, shooing them after answering the question of a man beneath him. His left hand is hidden under an opened book from them before they leave. The cut bleeds onto its pages, gluing the papers behind the most soaked ones. When alone enough, he pulls out the hand to take a look, not understanding the scribbles of the razor into it he caused in a haze of chemicals. The pinched face of his wonders more in silence while staring the red lines down that scratch the skin deeply. In the stare, the eyes drop a bit of agitation down, surrendering the judgement for now.

The heft following him about it, though, is substantial, like an overlooked dimension outside himself, as if a square ignored that it was part of the cube, or that the cube may be a part of the tesseract. The lapse… Then, the eye of a prick jolt, the churn of gut, all of it again, but a guilty pleasure along with the fear. His eyes want to puke it out, but he tries to take all that he can and force himself open. The flesh and mind of soul are split in half, like a seeing and a blindness dwelt in and out of simultuity. The smell of piss rushes from groin lived in but not his own, the rattle of a bottle, and the mirrors within mirrors in the dark. He would grip a little hard, yet the hands are in no one place but many lives. The feelings… More sights penetrate the intellect. He senses the brother named Lucifer sitting on his chest, the tank armor under rubble, the smack of a bull’s head blunting, and the bag full of gold paint in between clammy hands.

Kether opens his eyes, and they already meet the clock; not a minute has gone by. The form feels like unraveled shell, taken apart in groups like schematics undergone. It isn’t anything but happening to him. It is as if he is going to roll over, but the sluggish prism muddles the limbs into the feedback of melted clay. There is a crawling tingling inside his organs, swirling in rounds of itself with shivers of anticipation. The smile stretches over him, almost not his own. The walls bend through like wood rotting a century fast in seconds, the ceiling falling with it.

The hand wiggles around all at once, then not, taking turns with its rhythm. Eventually, Kether gives the needy limb attention, and his eyes burn at the sight. The back of hand begins moving open wounds with sounds, almost speaking through the boiling of hives: “I’m itself.”

Kether’s teeth clench, and his mouth opens like a carnivore while the back of neck tightens with his chest. The room gitties a little with mad joy of a galloping roundabout. The centrifugal vertigo rows the blood pressure in swells of narrow and gaped. The heart lunges into a rapid expanse, like two bulls playing tug of war with a long stretch of rope. It slings them backwards into each other when the exit—twine intertwines them, snatching them together with a shutter slam wrongly. The mouth burns dry at breathy excitement of bliss lungs heaved long. His bottom almost exhales with a stretch open as his bladder loosens like dead from the rush. Covered in sickly touch prickles that brush hard with their flinching terror blend.

While the ash of other death carousels flees the flash he indulges into like predator, the disappointment catches his sweet tooth hedonism reaching for another, and its roar like cornering. The mind of Kether is surrounded in moments of an angel catching him. The memories rush back, how he caught an angel he mocked, who called him the ancient poison. Kether falls out of his seat, sweeping the whole table down with all of its books. He smears the blood with flails, toppling with a head bobbling off ground’s hit. The fracture sells his nose’s structure with a fracturing currency earned. More hearty rushes spill as he clambers the ground towards the corner.

Kether looks at the clock, still not one minute… His mouth contorts into an expression of greedy relief. He rolls his head back a little, becoming wall, where the vision of a woman opens up a coffin, and it is… Him. The body he tries to jerk back is gone in the sight. The woman… The maiden she opens… Why does he see it from her sight? The torment stokes high, and the head struts strongly rearward; then the blunt has him found for a moment. The clock, still unmoving. The resolve is paled. Kether slumps over on the floor; wanting to deny.
