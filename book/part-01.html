No. 1

Ten: Dead Home

Calling me

That shaky whisper overcome by heavier breath

Weight in shakes and tightened chest

In my open ear, my ear almost moving from a sound not heard

That night screams

It's not safe here

Drunk on bridling poisons both caustic and bitter

Tortured with stirnament

Cornered into a fallscape retreating itself

Pulled out from the inside of corner

Curse waits for me there, saying to me,

“I need something made of you.”

Invisible warnings written on everything yet never heard

Now pulled into a blackened heat, fed ignorance

In a dead home whose walls are like an unmovable curtain

The draper hides something behind this stubborn myth
