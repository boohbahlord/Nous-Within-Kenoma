### Rules for every game

- R1 The Full Moon's "most powerful stack" demand can never pick a stack on (inside) an Ace.
- R2 Unlimited Miners and Repairmen ("builders") in the Ether; the Ether's shown total doesn't count them as infinite.
- R3 Selling to the Bank: price = the card's soul worth − 1d2 + 1 (i.e. worth minus 0 or 1), never more than it is worth; a
  standard card worth only 1 can't be sold to the Bank. The sell buttons in the Bank more obvious.
- R4 Rob the Bank (natural 20): steal as much as fits in the robber's soul pile, capped by what the Bank holds; can't be tried
  when the Bank holds 0 or less. (Replaces v39's "empties the Bank, overflow in a stash".)
- R5 Claimer (special, top-left "F", a flag symbol): one heart, no attack or defense; two per deck, waiting in the Ether by
  default. Its action "Claim Land" plants a Flag on its tile and the Claimer dies (its card goes straight back to the Ether).
  A Flag: not a card; three hearts (shown only when nobody stands on it), can be repaired; turns transparent under a stack like a
  crystal; a spawn point for bases. Every base except a Wall is a spawn point. Hovering a flag says so.
- R6 Lazy Hand (spell, free, white outline of a hand, listed right below the Bank, with an "auto" toggle like Grave Blessing):
  cast it, then click a card in your hand: the computer places it for you. With Field Automation on, it follows those settings.
- R7 Field Automation: support cards get their own context (they follow and serve the stacks they support).

### Help and onboarding

- H1 Top center of the play page: "What you must know" (blackletter, or the theme's display font), subtitle "Click me to know".
  It opens a panel: a TLDR (2–3 sentences: stay alive, get mana), a longer paragraph (miners, how to win, the Bank, planets with
  cycles and rare eclipses, the grave, bases and which cards they are, the deck as health and mana, sacrificing from the hand…),
  then "Tell me more here now" (goes deeper, and points to the in-game index at the bottom of the page).
  Above the TLDR: "Never show again" → confirm: Yes / No / Remind me at the start of the next game.
- H2 At the start of a game, a toast (not a window) points to the help index at the bottom. A small title "Help" above those
  fold-downs; it reads "Help & Configure Automation" when the automation panel is there.
