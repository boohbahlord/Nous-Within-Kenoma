## Part B — digest

### Music (first, by the user's word)
- M1 Render every song to an audio file, named by number, and send them here for the user's verdict. Then change them as told.

### Bugs and fixes
- F1 Grave automation's ranking has no ▸/▾ fold like the other automation menus.
- F2 King of the Hill: the Bank and Banker spawn inside the mountain ring; they belong outside it.
- F3 Large maps: the page can't be scrolled with the wheel while the mouse is over the field. The wheel must scroll the page by
  default; wheel zoom is an opt-in checkbox in a note under the field (with the controls listed).
- F4 Zoomed out (large maps, King of the Hill): card UI and tile names grow too big for their tiles and overlap. Cap what grows;
  shrink tile names; below a size, show only the card's main symbol in the middle. Check special animations too.
- F5 Dead space at the bottom of the page in King of the Hill / at 80% browser zoom (fix only if safe).

### Details
- T1 Ritual Dice playstyle names: plain, matter-of-fact names.
- T2 Fog drifting animation off by default.
- T3 The Debt Collector's "$" in the red used in the game's fonts.
- T4 Kind hierarchies (kinds of card only): drag and drop; Grunts and Infantry of every number fold into one (higher numbers
  are assumed first). Wildcards in automation kind lists leave out cards that can never be in the grave.
- T5 The spell list colored by school (the animation colors), Necromancy included, a little transparent.
- T6 The "Ash" color: something distinct. The player-setup menu: prettier, smaller, less scrolling. The "You" label on your
  seat: much more obvious.
- T7 The dice history: collapsible, and placed below the row of Grave / Auction / Ether / … buttons.
- T8 Configure Automation's summary: the statuses after the name smaller, to tell them apart.

### New content
- N1 The Wall can also be placed on any empty tile within a circle of 2 of a Repairman (the Repairman's tile shows blue when a
  Wall is selected); the Wall's and Repairman's card info say so.
- N2 Card faces: no top-left symbol; smaller suit symbols; cards in their player's color (less saturated, a darker tone, all
  text and art contrasting); the player's symbol off the cards unless a Display setting turns it on; Demons black with red;
  the Bank's cards a faint impression of paper money. Not every Display setting needs to sit in the bottom bar.
- N3 The color menu: each color comes with accent colors to choose.
- N4 Satan's symbol: real demon claws. Give the user an overview of Satan's abilities for brainstorming.
- N5 Carriages can be captured (the user's "maybe": treat as a proposal to confirm).
- N6 Automation: "Capture the Hill" (on by default in King of the Hill), with how: gather a big army first / straight there /
  in small groups. Attack grouping: big groups, small groups, or normal; formation (Wide, Tall) ties in. A more robust Field
  Automation and computer AI.
- N7 Cheat mode (off by default; "Are you sure?" with "Yes, I understand"; can be on in any mode; online rooms and the screen
  show it's on): a Cheat button by the Configure button, and a Cheat fold-down below Configure Automation (with its own on/off,
  on by default only for cheat players). Options: free magic (me / all), give everyone the cheat menu, infinite mana (me / all /
  the Banker), unlimited Ether, an event picker for rare events, add every spell (me / all) under a "Cheat List" school, block
  schools and single spells (pick a school first, then its spells), changes take effect next round (noted at the top), spawn
  Demons and other mobs, no victory or loss (and a player who runs out of cards starts again with a fresh deck).
- N8 1v1's default map 6×6. A map opacity setting (a few steps; at zero the map turns off), a Display option to turn the map
  off, and with no map the tiles that can't be played glow very faintly (no animation).
- N9 Stacks look like stacked cards, in many variations by roughly how many cards (not an exact count).

### Order (the user's): bugs first, details second, new content third, bugs last; UI/UX rules checked after every step.
