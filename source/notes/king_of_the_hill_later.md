## The digest (K1–K12, from the same sheet, Part B)

### King of the Hill and large maps (a new mode family)
- K1 Map: 25×25, night field, pixel art, brightens under the Second Sun; forests; mountains (void, unplayable) scattered and
  winding around the center with 2×2 gaps; a 5×5 white, translucent, glowing Hill in the middle, never fogged; spawns 2 deep × 3
  wide on every border and corner, with potions at their edges.
- K2 Players 2–8 (5–8 in the user's first line; any count ≥ 2 in team mode); Team mode on by default (teams need not be even);
  team colors on, each player's own color outlines the Hill square they hold.
- K3 Scoring: at the end of each round (after the attack phase) whoever holds the Hill gets +1 (two holders both score);
  targets 30 or 50; Marathon 100; Double points option; Contest option (a contested Hill scores for nobody); other ways to win
  still work. Tug of war (team mode, 25): one shared bar from −25 to +25; holding pushes it your way; contested stops it.
- K4 Marathon: holding the Hill can spawn mercenaries (like Barbarians, a crown for a symbol, in the team's color) on 1 of 1d4
  each turn, a large band, on the holder's team for good; every spawn announced with a toast to everyone.
- K5 On screen: the objective and a small score bar (or the tug-of-war bar).
- K6 Carriage (standard, top-left "13", carriage symbol; only in this mode): carries a stack 3 tiles in any direction;
  two hearts, no attack or defense; 1 mana; unlimited in the Ether (not counted); only Archers can shoot it; Archers can't ride.
  When it's destroyed, every card in it rolls 1d4 and dies on a 1; the rest stay stacked.
- K7 The Ether in this mode: starts with three decks' worth; every 10 turns one more deck's worth; no kind beyond four decks' worth.
- K8 Fog: sight is a circle of radius 2 (Scouts: a circle of 4, and they move 3 in any direction). Meteors: 5% every turn.
- K9 The Bank on the map: the Banker's card stands beside his base "Bank" (works like an Ace, can't die), placed near or outside
  the Hill, guarded by Debt Collectors on Carriages; more of them roam; debtors are hunted by carriages of collectors.
- K10 Camera (large maps only): wheel zoom; drag on empty board to pan; WASD/arrows pan (not while typing in chat); Q/E and C/V
  zoom; Shift pans faster; sensitivity setting. Menu tab "Large Map Controls" (faded outside large maps): invert wheel zoom,
  invert key zoom, wheel zoom off.
- K11 Setup: "King of the Hill" among the layouts; under it, Team mode (on) and a player count with arrows; Tug of war (hover
  explains), Double points, Contest; target 30 / 50 / Marathon 100; Headquarters still allowed; spawns "far enough but not as far
  as possible" by default, or "Spawn next to enemies".
- K12 New maps for every mode: 10×6 (1v1/2v2, sides on the short ends, teams share an end); 10×10 (free-for-all, 1v1, 2v2); a
  10×10 plus sign (arms 4 wide); and its variant with a round center (the hole option can open it). All of these use the large-map
  controls. The Map tab gets a "Large maps" group; choosing King of the Hill fades every other map out.
