A star breathed out in the demiurge's heaven to heal his angels.

The starch. 36

Back in his heaven after the moats, Yaldabaoth “breathes out a new Star. He names them “Redemption,” who he wills to heal any one of his three hundred and sixty-one angels who are willing to forgive themselves for shaking from wounds blessed unto them by God's will.” Three hundred and sixty are granted its healing. It undoes the stitches at their eyes and mouths, strengthens their bones, sutures their wings back onto them, and blesses the wings into flesh “with certainty in the name of the Demiurge, Yaldabaoth.”

The one it does not healch. 36

One angel denies the aid of Redemption. He is left wingless and broken, cast out of Heaven, and named Curse. See Curse and False Angels.
